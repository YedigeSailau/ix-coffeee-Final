A café for slow, snowy evenings

IX Coffee started as one roaster in a garage and a stubborn love of winter. We roast every Tuesday, bake every morning and light the garlands at sunset.

From December 1st the whole menu turns festive, and on the 31st the staff come to work in Santa hats. Yes, even the barista who says he hates dressing up.
